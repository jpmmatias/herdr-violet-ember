# Ember artwork

All PNG artwork is original and AI-generated. The vector character (`ember.svg` and `animation/`) is hand-written SVG. No third-party character assets are used.

## First portrait (not shipped)

An earlier waist-up portrait, `ember-v2.png`, was generated with the built-in image_gen tool, with a transparent background. The chibi below replaced it, and it is not included in this repository.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a polished original anime desktop companion called Ember for a violet coding terminal sidebar. Single waist-up portrait of a clearly adult anime woman, lilac layered hair with delicate pink streaks and a little flame hairclip, expressive violet eyes, mischievous friendly smile, oversized dark plum hoodie with lavender trim, small pink ribbon, holding a tiny coffee mug and making a playful peace sign. Premium contemporary anime game illustration, beautiful clean linework, rich cel shading, excellent face and hands, charming funny personality, not a crude vector cartoon. Centered complete head, hair and hands fully inside frame with padding, readable at 220px wide. Violet and blush palette, subtle cyan highlights. Genuine transparent background, no scene, no text, no logo, no watermark. Portrait aspect ratio.

The panel uses an illustrated portrait with native floating motion, not a Live2D rig. Click reactions and agent-status messages are local scripted text, not AI chat. The focus timer, coffee count, and interactions reset when the mod is closed or reloaded.
## Chibi revision

`ember-chibi.png` was generated with the built-in image_gen tool with transparency. Prompt:

Create one original full-body chibi anime coding companion Ember as a transparent PNG cutout for a desktop mascot. Tiny cute super-deformed adult character, head about 45 percent total height, entire body including boots fully visible and generous margin. Soft silvery lavender hair with pink tips, violet eyes, tiny flame hairclip, oversized dark purple hoodie, dark leggings, lilac ankle boots. Mischievous friendly closed-mouth smile, one hand waving, other at hip. Beautiful detailed modern anime game chibi illustration, clean lineart, soft cel shading. Standing relaxed front-facing, compact silhouette readable at 140 px tall. No background, no floor, no shadow backdrop, no text, no UI, no bubble, no extra characters. Genuine transparent background. Portrait canvas.

The layout now uses a draggable native floating block at the lower right. Herdr supplies its frame. Speech appears above the character; controls expand on demand. Sleep collapses to a wake button and does not pause the focus timer.
